# MaleCNS v1.0 derived model data

Source: MaleCNS project — FlyEM / HHMI Janelia, University of Cambridge,
MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology and Google Research.

Data and attribution: https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/
Citation: Berg et al. (2026), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.015
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes by Flybrain Playground: retain classified neurons, sort by body ID,
remap directed connections to CSR arrays, select sensory inputs and gzip the
arrays. No connection-weight cutoff or invented soma coordinates is applied.
Exact input source URLs and checksums are recorded in manifest.json. This
derived data remains CC BY 4.0; preserve this attribution when redistributing.
The data providers do not endorse the illustrative model or flight controller.
